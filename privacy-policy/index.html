Introduction

Pogo Hire ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in accordance with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Data We Collect

By using our website and submission form, you may provide us with
  • Name and Contact Information (Mobile/WhatsApp number).
  • Professional details (Area of expertise, work experience).
  • Residency status (Singapore Citizen/PR).

Purpose of Collection

We collect this data solely for the following purposes:
  • To evaluate your suitability for managed digital opportunities.
  • To communicate with you via WhatsApp or phone regarding opportunities.
  • To provide professional career consultancy and support.

Disclosure of Data

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Your profile is only shared with opportunities partners after we have had a personal consultation with you and obtained your verbal consent.

Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative and technical measures to ensure your data is kept confidential and secure at our CBD-based office.

Your Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal data, or withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our team at contact@pogohire.com.
